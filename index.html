Jiangwei Sui

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Electronics and Communication Engineering in Sun Yat-sen University, Shenzhen, from March 2025, promoted from an Assistant Professor from January 2021 to March 2025, and I work on antenna array, MIMO antennas, terminal antennas, antenna decoupling techniques, etc. Before that, I was with vivo, focused on smartphone antenna design and antenna decoupling research. I got my Ph.D. degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, in 2019, where I was supervised by Prof. Ke-Li Wu. And I got my bachelor's degree from University of Science and Technology of China, in 2014.

欢迎对新型导航通信系统、天线射频方向感兴趣、踏实、勤奋的同学联系我读研、毕设、大创等，一起研究探索天线和射频领域的前沿课题。本人更倾向于招收兴趣驱动、积极开朗、有团队协作精神的同学。
